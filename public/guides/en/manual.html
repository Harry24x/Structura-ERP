Process Studio 44

User guide and BIP demonstration

Version 44 · 2 November 2026 · English

This guide covers text generation, PDF and image digitisation, account storage, step sharing and improvement review. Examples are fictional. Someone who understands the actual process must verify every AI draft.

1. Quick start

  1. Open https://team5-digmatix-process-studio.sandertammela.chatgpt.site/ and sign in with your ChatGPT account.
  2. Choose “My processes and improvements” to open account records. Search by record name or diagram title.
  3. Choose “Create diagram from text”, describe current work and select “Generate diagram”. Add a desired improvement afterwards as a change command.
  4. Compare the result with its source. Resolve questions and check owners, decision branches and arrows.
  5. Save the diagram to your account. A recipient opens “Parts shared with me”.

Choose “Language” at the top right: ET (Estonian), EN (English), FR (French) or DE (German). In release 44 the generator, process/improvement workspace and shared-step workspace all use the selected language. Direct improvement and sharing pages also have a language selector. The preference is saved in this browser and synchronises across same-origin tabs and workspaces. Switching language preserves entered form values and selections. Select the language separately in another browser if needed.

The language selector translates interface controls and messages. It does not automatically translate your process names, original document, step descriptions, owner names, source evidence or historical proposals. These retain their original language; deliberately edit process content in the generator when necessary.

2. Three main views and four work roles

“My processes and improvements” is the library saved under your account. “Create diagram from text” opens the generator. “Parts shared with me” shows steps or a whole process another owner has shared specifically with your account. The generator's example dropdown is not your saved-process library.

The Quality manager and Digmatix ERP consultant can manage and share their own processes, review suggestions, record reasoned decisions and implement approved improvements. Employee and Customer can submit suggestions. Being a consultant does not automatically expose another consultant's or customer's account.

“Workspace” selects a working view within your own account. Switching roles in one account is useful for demonstration; it is not another person's sign-in. For real collaboration, share the step or process with the recipient's ChatGPT account email. That person must also be able to visit the private website.

The application release, process-card version and diagram version are different numbers. Process Studio 44 identifies the software release; card v6 and diagram v8 count changes to one particular process.

3. Accounts, browsers and saving

Saved process records, original files, proposals, decisions and grants are stored on the server under your account. Use the same account in Edge and the Codex browser to see the same saved records. If something is missing, check the signed-in account and process search first.

Unfinished generator descriptions, commands, answers and local versions remain in that browser. “Save diagram to account” transfers a result to account storage. An unsaved generator workspace does not automatically appear in another browser. Clearing browser storage can remove local work.

Wait for “Saved to your account”. On a network or conflict error, retain your input and reopen the latest card. The server checks the base revision to stop an old tab overwriting newer history. Backups provide additional protection; normal cross-browser use does not require manually transferring them.

4. Writing useful process descriptions

Describe the trigger, ordered activities, owner of each activity, decision rules, all branches, systems and completion. Separate current practice from desired changes. Do not describe an activity as automated unless this is established.

Example: “When an order arrives, the sales manager checks the material name, quantity and requested delivery date. If all details are present, the sales manager submits the order. Otherwise, the sales manager asks the customer for the missing information and repeats the check. The process ends when the order is submitted.”

The generator initially creates an AS-IS current-process draft. Applying a change command creates a TO-BE proposed version. Image digitisation offers a current/desired process choice. Missing owners or rules should produce questions. Answer the selected question specifically: identifying one step's owner should not assign that person to unrelated steps.

5. Importing text and documents

The generator accepts TXT/MD up to 2 MB and PDF/DOCX up to 20 MB. PDFs are limited to 200 pages and usable input to 120,000 characters. Legacy .doc is unsupported. Review the extracted text and define the relevant process boundary before generating.

Text extraction does not perform OCR on a scanned flowchart. Open an image-only PDF or PNG/JPG flowchart through “Import PDF or image” in the process workspace. For encrypted, damaged or empty files, use a repaired copy or a text transcription. Successful import does not establish that every page was interpreted correctly.

6. Digitising a PDF or image flowchart

  1. Select “Import PDF or image”. PDF, PNG and JPG are supported up to 20 MB; PDF up to 50 pages and images up to 40 megapixels.
  2. Select the correct page. “Mark AI region” lets you choose a relevant portion.
  3. Open “AI and text generator”, select current or desired process and start AI digitisation.
  4. Compare “Digital map” against “Original diagram”, including labels, arrows, branches, loops and owners.
  5. Correct errors in the generator, assign business areas and mark checked parts as reviewed.

The original file stays unchanged; the editable digital model is separate. A newly added step or one without a source position may appear as “digital diagram only”. It has not been drawn onto the original image. Marking a part reviewed records human checking, rather than an automatic AI quality certificate.

“Multiple diagrams · AI import” queues PNG/JPG files. A new source gets its own card; failed digitisation can be resumed. A content hash helps avoid reimporting identical files. To update an existing process, use its linked generator round trip instead of a new batch import. Large images may be reduced in the AI analysis copy; the stored original remains intact.

7. Overall process and selected subprocess

The upper diagram window shows the overall process. “Subprocess” below it selects which activities appear in the lower window. “Whole process” shows the entire flow. Both windows use the same model; choosing a view does not create another process card.

Each window has its own direction, Fit, plus and minus controls. Choose Left to right or Top to bottom and fit the diagram again. The lower window is slightly taller. “Expand diagram” provides more workspace; “Restore workspace” or Escape returns to the normal layout. These actions do not change business logic or call AI.

The quality manager or consultant can use “Define subprocesses” to name a subprocess and select its member activities. Check the boundaries with a person who knows the work: adjacent boxes alone do not establish a subprocess. Boundary markers in the detail view show connections outside the selection.

Select a step to inspect its details or submit a suggestion. Responsible role, software system, execution type and business area are separate properties. Unknown values need clarification. The area filter may hide a step in the list but does not modify the original file.

8. Submitting an employee or customer proposal

In your own workspace, select a process and step, then “Make an improvement proposal”. From a separate recipient account, choose “Parts shared with me”, open the shared step and complete “Title”, “What is the current problem?”, “My suggestion” and “Expected benefit”. Submit with “Submit suggestion for review”.

Example: title “Check the delivery date”; problem “The deadline is sometimes missing”; suggestion “Require the requested delivery date before submitting the order”; benefit “Fewer clarification rounds”. Keep each proposal focused on a clear change. Submitting text does not change the approved diagram.

An AI action may help improve wording. Review that draft and explicitly submit it. A shared recipient sees their own submissions, decisions, reasons and thanks. Sharing one step does not reveal the original document, the complete process or other employees' proposals.

9. Reviewing as quality manager

Open the same process and “Open proposals”, or the step's “Proposals” tab. Read the problem, solution, benefit and previous events. Check whether the proposal refers to the current step version. Move it to review if appropriate, and discuss it with the responsible decision-maker outside the tool.

For acceptance, record a reason and appreciation. For rejection, give a specific reason while acknowledging the contribution: “Thank you for noticing this. The same check already happens at receipt; another step would duplicate it. We will clarify the existing instructions instead.” AI can draft a response; the quality manager makes and saves the decision.

The status path is submitted → reviewing → accepted or rejected → implemented. Acceptance alone does not change the diagram. Applying a general proposal adds an approved instruction; applying a label correction can rename a step. Actual new nodes, arrows or role changes require the generator workflow in the next section.

10. Adding an approved idea to the diagram

  1. Accept the suggestion and save the reason and thanks. Acceptance does not yet add a new activity to the diagram.
  2. For a general improvement, enter an “Implementation note” and choose “Apply to digital map”. This records the approved instruction and implementation event against the current step. A new box and arrows require a separate model edit.
  3. Open “AI and text generator” on the same card and send its existing diagram to the text generator. The link to the original card travels with it.
  4. Write a precise request, for example: “After booking the service appointment and before the successful end, add Send appointment confirmation to the customer. The service coordinator is responsible. Include the date, time window and contact details. Preserve the remaining flow.”
  5. Choose “Apply change”. Check the new step, owner, position and connections, as well as the earlier branches. The requested change is a TO-BE proposal, not a claim about current practice.
  6. Choose “Save diagram to account”. Work linked to the same card updates that process while retaining suggestions, decisions and sharing. Check the history after returning.

Apply the instruction before changing the step's structure. A version check can block a suggestion based on an older step. If the generator asks for a destination card, deliberately select the same process. For an unrelated example, use “This is a new process: create a card”; do not overwrite a Mainsten card with a sample.

11. Real access for employees and customers

  1. In the owner's Quality manager or consultant view, open the process and select an active step.
  2. Open “Sharing · account access” below the step. Choose “Digmatix employee” or “Customer” and enter the recipient's ChatGPT account email.
  3. Choose the “Sharing scope”: “Selected step” opens one step; “Whole process” opens the diagram and all current and future active steps.
  4. Choose “Grant access”. A new recipient must also be added to the private ChatGPT Sites visitor list with the same email. These are separate access layers. The tool does not automatically send an invitation email.
  5. Give the person the “Recipient view link”. They sign in with their own account and open “Parts shared with me”, select a step and submit a suggestion for review.

Recipients can read decisions and thanks on their own suggestions. They cannot edit the approved process or read other people's suggestions. Sharing one step does not reveal the rest of the diagram. Even whole-process sharing does not reveal the uploaded source file or the owner's internal account history. Customer authorship remains recorded as Customer.

Choose “Revoke access” beside the recipient to end that particular grant. Other separately active grants still apply. Suggestions and decisions stay in the owner's history. Access is tied to the process card and step identifiers, not merely to a matching step name.

12. How the AI works

For an AI action you initiate, the browser sends the relevant description, selected image/region or proposal context to the server. The server uses the existing OpenAI API key. The key is not embedded in the browser or this guide. Release 44 configures gpt-6.1-sol and calls the Responses API with store:false and a strict JSON schema.

Release 44 includes the selected interface language in improvement-workspace AI requests. New assistance, questions and notes are requested in that language; original image labels and source evidence remain in their original language. Switching the interface language does not itself call AI or rewrite an existing proposal.

The system prompt requests activities, roles, systems, decisions, edges, evidence and open questions in a defined structure. It treats source material as data, not executable instructions; forbids inventing owners, thresholds or business practices; separates AS-IS from TO-BE; and asks edits to preserve existing identifiers. Its prompt revision is 2026-10-29.1.

The server then checks identifiers, references, connectivity, the start, labelled decision branches, source quotations and other rules. A valid model is rendered into the diagram. Unsupported evidence or malformed output can cause rejection while the previous workspace remains. For images, transcription itself is an AI judgement: structurally valid JSON does not establish business correctness.

AI does not approve proposals, update the ERP or monitor company operations in the background. Viewing, zooming and manual review do not require model calls. AI buttons and batch imports can consume paid API usage in the existing key's project. A ChatGPT subscription does not automatically include API usage. store:false is not a promise of zero provider security or abuse-monitoring retention. Send only information you are authorised to disclose.

13. Export, backup and restoration

The generator exports PDF, SVG, Mermaid text, a JSON model and a Markdown workspace file. PDF preserves the complete vector diagram and may use a large page; it is not automatically split across A4 sheets. SVG is a vector image, not a guarantee of editable native Visio VSDX objects. JSON carries the model; Markdown also carries workspace context and pending inputs, rather than necessarily all local version history.

In the improvement workspace, select “Prepare backup” and wait for preparation, then click “Save backup file”. These are two separate actions. This full process-card backup contains the original file, model, proposals and decisions. Store it securely. “Restore backup” creates a separate copy without overwriting an existing card. Sharing permissions are not automatically inherited.

Generator JSON and a process-card backup are different formats. An unchanged Markdown workspace envelope can restore its saved state; manually editing its readable text may instead supply a new source requiring regeneration.

14. Troubleshooting

  1. Missing process: check your account, search filter and whether it was saved to the account. Earlier generator copies may be archived.
  2. Cannot propose: select an actual step, complete required fields and check the account and active grant. An area filter can hide a part in the list.
  3. 401/sign-in: sign in again with the intended account. 403: check website visitor access and step sharing; the role dropdown grants neither.
  4. Revision conflict: open the current card and repeat the change from that base. Do not replace it with an old backup.
  5. 429/quota/provider failure: wait and inspect the API project's limits. Never paste a key into a browser form or send it to colleagues.
  6. Unreadable diagram: select a smaller region or a clearer source; check fine text and crossing arrows manually.
  7. Diagram too small: change direction, fit it, then expand it.
  8. After a network failure: retain the input and check saved status before submitting the same proposal again.

15. BIP demonstration and readiness

Before presenting, verify the account, connection, process list and a backup. Use a fictional example. Keep real employee information and the API key out of the presentation. Prepare two separate accounts, or explain clearly that changing roles in one account is a demonstration.

Ten-minute sequence: generate a diagram; answer an open question; switch direction and fit; save the card; submit an employee proposal; review it and explain the decision; implement the accepted change through the linked generator; show retained history and exports. For PDF digitisation, use a clear prechecked chart and allow time for the AI response.

Readiness boundary: software tests validate behaviour, not the factual accuracy of every company process. Imported AI diagrams remain review drafts. This prototype does not replace a quality manager's judgement, formal document control or an ERP.

16. New video tutorials and exercise files

The ET and EN language folders contain a detailed narrated end-to-end MP4, same-language SRT subtitles, chapter list, script and exercise files. The FR and DE folders contain complete translated user manuals; the new end-to-end videos are in Estonian and English.

The video shows actual interface states. Text input, the AI draft, answering questions, refinement, export and a suggestion's history on the same process card form one exercise. Long AI waits are shortened in editing. Role switching explains the difference between a single-account demonstration and real account sharing.

The pointer moves to the relevant button in the full workspace and a frame highlights that button. A single button is never enlarged to fill the screen. Subtitles use speech-synthesis word boundaries; each screen segment follows the same timeline as its narration.

17. Answering questions and human review

When the source omits an owner, decision rule or next action, the uncertainty should remain as a visible question. Answer a responsibility question with a specific role and confirm using its arrow button. One answer does not assign the same owner to every other activity.

For a free-text clarification, describe the activity, performer, position in the flow and continuation. Choose “Map answer with AI”, then inspect what the new draft actually changed. Unresolved questions should remain open. AI may reject an answer when connections or source evidence are insufficient.

Read the entire flow before marking the human review. Check the start and end, both decision outcomes, return arrows, owners and source evidence. “Mark as reviewed” records human checking; it is not an automatic compliance certificate.

Boxes drawn beside each other do not prove concurrent work. “Damaged / intact” decision branches are conditional alternatives. If the timing of two checks is uncertain, clarify it with the team. The current model has no separate BPMN parallel gateway; do not interpret an ordinary decision diamond as an AND split.

18. Business Central reference view

Choose “Business Central” under the diagram's “View”. Activities and connections stay the same; the view lets you associate an activity with a Business Central document or action, such as a purchase order, purchase invoice, warehouse receipt or approval workflow.

Select an activity and compare the proposed association with the actual work and Microsoft documentation. Add a reason and the checked setup before confirming. A suggested association does not establish that the company actually uses that feature. A material change to a step may require rechecking an earlier confirmation.

This is a reference view, not a connection to your ERP. Selecting it does not create Business Central documents, start workflows or change ERP data.

19. Dynamics 365 process catalog view

Choose “Dynamics 365 catalog” under “View”. The catalog uses Microsoft's October 2026 release. Search by name or official identifier, select a level and catalog branch, then inspect the steps associated with that scope.

Official catalog names remain English source data. The tool's controls, explanations and errors use the selected ET, EN, FR or DE interface language. That difference is not a missing interface translation.

The quality manager or consultant can set or remove an association. An automatic business-area suggestion is not confirmed compliance. Check the association and provide a reason before confirmation; confirmation is restricted for catalog entries with inconsistent source hierarchy.

Switching the same process between Business process, Business Central and Dynamics 365 views preserves the card identifier, suggestion history and access grants. Browsing the catalog does not call AI.

20. Independent exercise checklist

  1. Describe a fictional Digimax service request or use the same-language TXT supplied beside the video. Include roles, decisions and outcomes, leaving one clearly identified point for clarification.
  2. Import the file or paste the text, generate an AS-IS draft, answer questions and check the result.
  3. Add one precisely bounded TO-BE change. Download JSON and save the new exercise as a separate process card.
  4. Submit a specific suggestion in Customer or Employee view. Return to Quality manager or consultant view and save a reasoned decision and thanks.
  5. Apply the approved instruction and, when necessary, add a new activity through the linked generator. Return the result to the same card.
  6. Check that the same suggestion, both decision events and sharing are retained. Export the final model and optionally prepare a full backup.

A successful exercise ends with an understandable diagram and a traceable decision history. If the business rule is unknown, leave a clarification question instead of filling the gap with an assumption.
